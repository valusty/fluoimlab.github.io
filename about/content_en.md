# About the Laboratory

Welcome to **FIL** — the Fluorescence Imaging Laboratory.

## Our Mission

FIL specializes in the development and application of modern fluorescence microscopy methods for the study of biological systems. We combine cutting-edge imaging technologies with innovative image analysis approaches to address fundamental and applied scientific challenges.

## Research Areas

- **Fluorescence Microscopy** — confocal, widefield, and epi-fluorescence live-cell microscopy
- **Image Analysis** — development of software tools for microscopy image processing and analysis
- **Calcium Imaging** — investigation of intracellular calcium dynamics
- **Spectral Analysis** — spectral unmixing and linear deconvolution methods
