# Project Structure: Fluorescence Imaging Laboratory (FIL)

## Overview
A high-end digital presence for a state-of-the-art microscopy core facility. The site must bridge the gap between academic authority and the breathtaking visual beauty of cellular imaging.

## Target Audience
- Principal Investigators (PIs) looking for advanced imaging modalities.
- Postdoctoral researchers and PhD students requiring equipment training.
- Global collaborators reviewing the lab's scientific output and publications.

## Primary User Flows
1. **Explore Lab Mission:** High-impact "About" page establishing institutional prestige.
2. **Review Capabilities:** Browsing equipment and imaging services (Confocal, STED, etc.).
3. **Scientific Proof:** Accessing the research database and publication gallery.
4. **Professional Growth:** Checking the academic calendar for workshops and seminars.

---

## Screen Inventory

### 1. Home / About (Index)
- **Purpose:** Brand landing and mission statement.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Hero: Full-bleed "Illuminating the Matrix of Life" visual.
  - Metrics: Grid showing "15+ Modalities", "24/7 Access", "50nm Resolution".
  - Mission Statement: Academic-style typography explaining the facility's role in advancing biological research.
  - Navigation: Header with Research, Equipment, Services, Team, and Events.

### 3. Equipment & Services
- **Purpose:** Technical inventory of lab hardware.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Sidebar: Navigation between modalities (Confocal, Live-Cell, Super-Resolution, Analysis).
  - Detail View: Large sample image (e.g., neural network), technical summary, and system capabilities list.
  - Filter Bar: System types (Confocal, Super-Res, Widefield, etc.).
  - Visuals: Dark-field product photography of microscopes (Stellaris 8, Lightsheet Z.1).
  - Navigation: Header with Research, Equipment, Services, Team, and Events.

### 4. Our Research
- **Purpose:** Showcase academic output.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Filters: Sidebar for Year, Technique, and Fluorophore.
  - Publication Feed: List of papers with DOI links, journals (Cell, Nature Methods), and high-resolution microscopy thumbnails.
  - Dataset Gallery: Grayscale preview of raw scientific data.

### 5. Team
- **Purpose:** Directory of imaging specialists.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Tabular List: Clean, data-driven rows showing Name, Role ([DIRECTOR], [SENIOR_SCIENTIST]), Specialty, and ORCID link.
  - Portraits: Monochrome or high-contrast small avatar images to maintain professional tone.
  - Navigation: Header with Research, Equipment, Services, Team, and Events.

### 6. Events / Education
- **Purpose:** Training and community workshops.
- **Key Sections:**
  - Academic Calendar: Vertical feed of upcoming events.
  - Event Cards: Date-focused (OCT 14, NOV 15), Status tags ([OPEN], [WAITLIST], [FULL]), and Registration CTAs.
  - Location/Time: Clear metadata for physical auditorium vs virtual sessions.
  - Navigation: Header with Research, Equipment, Services, Team, and Events.

---

## Navigation Strategy
- **Top Bar:** Fixed, minimalist, containing the "FIL" wordmark, primary links, and a high-contrast "Connect" button.
- **Footer:** Academic standard, featuring Legal Documents, Ethics, Privacy Policy, Data Governance, and copyright info.

## Content Guidelines
- **Imagery:** High-contrast fluorescence microscopy composites (ACTIN, TUBULIN, NUCLEI colors).
- **Copy:** Professional, precise, and authoritative. Use terms like "Imaging Modalities," "Sub-diffraction resolution," and "Quantitative Proteomics."
