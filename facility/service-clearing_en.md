Clearing makes a fixed sample transparent to light, so it can be imaged whole,
without mechanical sectioning. The procedure is chosen to match the sample type
and the labelling method.

## Available protocols

### OptiMuS

:::note
Can be used for prestained tissues. Already in use in our laboratory for nerve and brain tissue. 
:::

Histodenz solution (a.k.a. Iohexol) was prepared by dissolving 75% (w/v) histodenz in Tris-EDTA solution at 60 °C until totally transparent. After complete dissolution, 10% (w/v) D-sorbitol and 4 M urea were subsequently dissolved in the mixture at 60 °C. The solution was then cooled to RT (room temperature).
All clearing procedures were performed at 37 °C and the solution was stored at 4 °C for later use.

Source: [Kim et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03388-8)

### CUBIC

:::note
Can be used for prestained tissues. Already in use in our laboratory for nerve and dorsal root ganglia (DRG) tissue. 
:::

**For clearing**: CUBIC-L is a mixture of 10% (wt/wt) N-butyldiethanolamine and 10% (wt/wt) Triton X-100 in ddH2O. to prepare 100 g of CUBIC-L solution, mix 10 g of N-butyldiethanolamine and 10 g of Triton X-100 in 80 g of ddH2O with a stirrer at room temperature (22–26 °C). Half-diluted CUBIC-L is an equivalent-volume mixture of CUBIC-L and dH2O. 

**For RI matching**: CUBIC-R+(N) is a mixture of 45% (wt/wt) antipyrine, 30% (wt/wt) nicotinamide, 0.5% (vol/vol) N-butyldiethanolamine and ddH2O. To prepare 100 g of CUBIC-R+ solution, dissolve 45 g of antipyrine and 30 g of nicotinamide in 25 g of ddH2O. After complete dissolution, add 250 μl of N-butyldiethanolamine to 50 ml of the mixture so that the pH becomes 9.6–9.8 and the RI is 1.522.

Source: [Matsumoto  et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03388-8)

### Scale

:::note
For samples labelled with fluorescent proteins, as well for prestained samples. Already have all the solutions, but we haven’t used it in our lab.
:::

**ScaleA2**: samples transferred in 4 M urea, 10% (wt/vol) glycerol, and 0.1% (vol/vol) Triton X-100  and incubated at 25 °C with gentle shaking for 21 d.

**ScaleU2**: samples transferred in 4 M urea, 30% glycerol, and 0.1% Triton X-100 and incubated at 25 °C with gentle shaking for 21 d.

**ScaleSQ(0) solution**: 22.5% (w/v) sorbitol, 9.1 M urea in ddH2O. For 200-µm thick rat brain slice clearing, ScaleSQ(0) solution was treated for 2 min at 37 °C. For 1-mm thick rat brain clearing, samples were immersed in ScaleSQ(0) for 1 h at 37 °C.


Source: [Hama et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2928), [Ke et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3447), [Kim et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03388-8)
