## Access Procedure

:::steps
### Contact us
Describe your research task, sample type and preferred timeline.

### Prepare the samples
We provide guidance on sample preparation, labelling and storage.

### Schedule the experiment
Instrument time is booked in advance.

### Receive the results
Data are delivered in open formats together with acquisition metadata.
