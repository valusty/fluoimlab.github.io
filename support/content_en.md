and hospitals are among the targets. Every donation helps Ukraine defend
freedom, democracy and its right to exist.

:::links
### United24
url: https://u24.gov.ua/
meta: Official platform

The official fundraising platform of Ukraine, launched by the President of
Ukraine. Supports defence, medical aid and reconstruction.

### Come Back Alive
url: https://savelife.in.ua/en/
meta: Defence

One of the largest Ukrainian foundations supporting the Armed Forces of
Ukraine with equipment, training and technology.

### Serhiy Prytula Charity Foundation
url: https://prytulafoundation.org/en
meta: Defence and humanitarian aid

Provides equipment for the military and humanitarian assistance for civilians
in de-occupied and front-line regions.
:::

## How else you can help

:::steps
### Donate
Any amount matters. The foundations above publish regular reports on how the
funds are spent.

### Share verified information
Refer to primary sources and Ukrainian institutions when writing or speaking
about the war.

### Support Ukrainian science
Invite Ukrainian researchers to collaborate, provide access to equipment,
literature and travel grants.
:::

:::note
The list of foundations is maintained by the laboratory team. To suggest a
change, please contact us.
:::
