# Services

FIL provides fluorescence imaging and image analysis services for research and educational institutions.

## Microscopy Services

### Confocal Microscopy
Confocal microscopy of fixed and live samples.

- Z-stack acquisition and 3D reconstruction
- Time-lapse imaging of dynamic processes
- Multi-channel fluorescence imaging

### Widefield Fluorescence Microscopy
Standard epi-fluorescence microscopy for routine studies.

- Visualization of fluorescently labeled samples
- Immunocytochemistry documentation
- Cell viability assessment
